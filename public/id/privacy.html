Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our service:

  • Discord account information (username, email, avatar) via OAuth
  • Roblox account credentials (User ID, API Key) that you provide
  • Audio conversion history and upload preferences
  • Payment information processed securely through Midtrans

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account via Discord OAuth
  • To convert and upload audio files to your Roblox account
  • To process subscription payments
  • To provide customer support

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Encrypted database connections (MongoDB Atlas)
  • Encrypted Roblox credentials using AES-256
  • Secure file storage (Cloudflare R2)
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Discord: For authentication (OAuth)
  • Roblox: For audio upload via official Open Cloud API
  • Midtrans: For payment processing (QRIS) - PCI DSS compliant
  • MongoDB Atlas: For secure database hosting
  • Cloudflare: For file storage (R2)

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Request complete data deletion
  • Unlink your Roblox accounts instantly
  • Cancel your subscription without penalties
  • Export your conversion history
